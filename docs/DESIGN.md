# Design Direction

## Users

Antelope Synergy Core owners controlling connected speakers and headphones
during music production, mixing, monitoring, and live-performance workflows.
The primary job is to adjust listening volume faster than opening and operating
Antelope's Control Panel.

## Brand Personality

Precise, industrial, and confident. The interface should feel dependable and
immediate, like dedicated monitor-controller hardware rather than a general
purpose dashboard.

## Aesthetic Direction

A compact, dark, high-contrast macOS utility with a strong hardware-controller
character. It should remain readable at a glance, minimise visual noise, and
avoid generic neon gaming or AI-dashboard aesthetics.

## Design Principles

1. Make speaker and headphone volume control the fastest and most prominent interaction.
2. Prioritise immediate feedback, reliability, and safe output identification over decoration.
3. Keep essential controls glanceable and directly accessible; progressively disclose secondary settings.
4. Preserve a compact industrial visual language with strong hierarchy and restrained accent colour.
5. Target WCAG AA where applicable, including keyboard access, visible focus, adequate control targets, and reduced-motion support.
